 In this work we will present a method to find $M$-soliton solutions to the classical Calogero-Moser (CM) systems. The CM model has been studied extensively  and occupies a prominent place among integrable systems, a rapidly developing branch of modern mathematical physics and beyond, at the crossroads of many areas of mathematics and theoretical physics. \\
 F. Calogero introduced and solved first in a quantum context the 1-dimensional CM system with arbitrary $N$ in \cite{calogero71}. The analogous model on the circle was treated independently by Bill Sutherland in \cite{sutherland}. C. Marchioro studied the classical analogue on the line \cite{marchioro1970, marchioro1974}. In 1975 J. Moser proved that the classical CM system is integrable for arbitrary $N$ \cite{moser1975}. \\
 In this thesis we will start by providing the necessary background of Hamiltonian mechanics in Chapter~\ref{ch:hamiltonian}. Then, we will review a few important mathematical tools of modern theory of integrable systems, such as Lax representation and Bäcklund transformations. In Chapter~\ref{ch:cm} we will define the classical Calogero-Moser systems and present some of their properties. We will show how they can be arrived at by means of the Kazhdan-Kostant-Sternberg (KKS) construction and how solutions can be obtained through the Olshanetsky-Perelomov (OP) projection method. We will exhibit their Lax representations and their Bäcklund transformations, from which we will derive soliton equations in Chapter~\ref{ch:solitons}.
 We will deal with soliton solutions of systems of Calogero-Moser type. We will define what exactly a soliton solution to an $N$-body problem is, and derive these solutions in a unified way for the systems under analysis, following \cite{philip2019soliton}. Lastly, we will study numerically soliton solutions for the rational case with external harmonic potential (eCM), as done in \cite{kulkarni2017, abanov2011}, for different values of $N$.
